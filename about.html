Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers

What is ShambaNi?

ShambaNi is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers across East Africa. We eliminate middlemen to ensure farmers get fair prices and buyers get fresh produce at affordable rates.

Which countries do you operate in?

We currently operate in 4 East African countries: Uganda (111 districts), Kenya (47 counties), Tanzania (31 regions), and Rwanda (30 districts).

How do I become a seller?

Simply go to the Farmer Registration page and complete our 4-step wizard. You'll need to provide personal information, farm details, and payment setup. Approval usually takes 24-48 hours.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept PayPal (@LutwamaReagan), Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, and Bank Transfer. All payments are secure and processed instantly.

Is there a platform fee?

Yes, we charge a small 2.5% platform fee on each transaction to maintain the marketplace, provide customer support, and ensure secure payments. This is one of the lowest rates in the industry.

How is delivery handled?

Buyers can choose between farm pickup or doorstep delivery. We partner with local logistics providers to ensure timely delivery. Delivery is free for orders above UGX 50,000.
