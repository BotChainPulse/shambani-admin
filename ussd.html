Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our USSD service

Does USSD *220# work on any phone?

Yes! USSD *220# works on any mobile phone - from the latest smartphones to basic feature phones. All you need is a mobile network connection. No internet or data plan is required.

How much does it cost to use *220#?

You only pay standard USSD rates charged by your network provider (typically UGX 20-50 per session). ShambaNi does not charge any additional fees for USSD access.

Which mobile networks are supported?

ShambaNi USSD works on all major networks across East Africa including MTN Uganda, Airtel Uganda, MTN Rwanda, Airtel Rwanda, Safaricom Kenya, Airtel Kenya, Vodacom Tanzania, Airtel Tanzania, and more.

Can I pay using Mobile Money through USSD?

Yes! When you place an order through *220#, you'll receive a Mobile Money payment prompt on your phone. You can pay using MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money directly from the USSD session.

Is the USSD service available in my language?

Yes! ShambaNi USSD is available in 4 languages: English, Kiswahili, Runyarwanda, and Luganda. You can select your preferred language from the main menu or settings.

How do I register as a farmer using USSD?

Select option 5 "Register as Farmer" from the main menu. Follow the prompts to enter your name, phone number, district, and farm details. Registration takes less than 2 minutes!
